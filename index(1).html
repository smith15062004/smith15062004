Our Process

From First Sketch to Final Key.

A clear five-stage journey, so you always know what happens next.

01
Consult

Requirement Gathering

Site soil assessment, sunlight analysis, family lifestyle workshops, and transparent budget scoping.

02
Design

Planning & Design

3D photorealistic elevations, MEP coordination, Vaastu alignments, and government sanction approvals.

Build
